how it works

What a page can hear.

The honest part first

Your phone’s always-on wake word belongs to Siri or Google Assistant. No web page, this one included, can listen while the screen is locked or while you are in another app. “Hey Siri, open Claude” already works today without any of this.

What this page adds is three things.

1. A listener, while it is open

Tap the microphone and talk. Keep the page open on the counter, in the car mount, on the desk, and “Hey GPT” opens ChatGPT, “Hey NYT” opens the Times, “Hey Maker, …” makes an app. Recognition is the browser’s own; nothing you say is sent to this site.

2. Links a shortcut can open

Every phrase has a link, https://pflantzdog27.github.io/hey-test/go/claude and so on, that sends a phone to the right place. Give that link to a Siri shortcut or an Assistant routine named after the phrase and the phrase works with the screen locked. Each phrase on the front page has the steps.

3. A maker

“Hey Maker, make me something to help my son study, I’ll upload some pictures.” The sentence goes to a model that picks from a fixed set of pieces (cards, checklist, timer, prompts, photo slots) and fills them in. It cannot write code or markup. You are asked whether to delete the result in 3, 7, 14 or 30 days, and on that day the record simply stops existing. With the model unavailable you get the standard kit, labelled as such.

With the screen locked: a Siri shortcut named Maker with Dictate text followed by Open URLs pointed at https://pflantzdog27.github.io/hey-test/make?brief=[Dictated Text] opens the form with your sentence filled in.

Where things live

  • Your own phrases: in this browser’s storage. Never sent anywhere.
  • Pictures you add to a made app: in this browser’s storage. Never uploaded.
  • Made apps: on this site, under an unguessable link, until the day you picked.
  • Who can change a made app: the browser that made it, via a cookie, or whoever holds the key the API returned.

For agents

Everything on this page is callable: /api for JSON, /mcp for tools, /llms.txt for the map. An agent can parse a phrase, make an app, set its expiry and delete it, exactly as a person can.
