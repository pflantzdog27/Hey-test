hey · a spoken phrase book

Say the word.

A spoken phrase book for your phone. Say “Hey Claude” and Claude opens; say “Hey Maker” and describe an app, and one is made for you that deletes itself when you are done with it.

listen

Voice needs JavaScript and a browser that can hear (Safari on iPhone, Chrome on Android). Every phrase below also works with a tap.

the phrase book

Out of the box

Each phrase opens one thing. The note under it says exactly what happens, because a page cannot put your phone into another app’s voice mode; it can only open the app.

  • “Hey GPT” opens ChatGPT

    Opens the ChatGPT app when it is installed, otherwise chatgpt.com. Voice mode is one tap from there; ChatGPT publishes no link that starts it directly.

    Open ChatGPT

    make it work with the screen locked

    A web page cannot listen while your phone is in your pocket; only Siri and Google Assistant can. So give them the phrase and point it at this link:

    https://pflantzdog27.github.io/hey-test/go/gpt

    1. iPhone. Shortcuts → + → name it Hey GPT → add the action Open URLs and paste the link. Then say “Siri, Hey GPT”.
    2. Android. Google Assistant → Routines → New → starter When I say “Hey GPT” → add the action Try adding your own and type open https://pflantzdog27.github.io/hey-test/go/gpt.

yours

Your own phrases

Any phrase, any link. “Hey Maps” to a maps link, “Hey Bedtime” to a playlist. They are kept in this browser only and are never sent anywhere.

  • Adding a phrase needs JavaScript, because the list lives in your browser.

hey maker

Make an app

Describe it in a sentence. You get one page with the pieces it needs — cards, a checklist, a timer, photo slots — a link to open it, and a phrase to open it by voice. On the day you pick, it deletes itself.

delete it in

One page, no account, gone on the day you picked.
